## 1.5 Create Annotation Schema ##
### 1.5.1 Define Classes ###

1. Click on Markables (The darker one)

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-1.png)

2. Click on the icon ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-2.png) to open Class Manager. 

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-3.png)

   You will see the dialog of "Class Manager" pop up

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-4.png)
 

3. Click on button of ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-5.png) to  enter "Problem" for the new class, and select color from drop-down list.

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-6.png)


4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 to add additional classes.

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-7.png)

5. Click when finished. 

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/1.5.1-8.png)
