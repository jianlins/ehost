### 2.5.4 Add/Delete/Modify Relationships
**2.5.4.1	Add Relationships**
Check **[1.6.3](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/wiki/1.6.3-Create-Relationship)**

**2.5.4.2	Delete Relationships**
1. Click ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/2.5.4-1.png) to open Relationship Schema Editor.

2. Select the relationship.

3. Click “Remove Selected” to delete relationship.to open Attribute Editor

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/2.5.4-2.png)


**2.5.4.3	Modify Relationship**
1. Click ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/2.5.4-3.png) to open Relationship Schema Editor.

2. Select the relationship.

3. Click “Edit” to edit.

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/2.5.4-4.png)

4. Type in new Regular Expression and new Relationship Name. 

5. Click “Save” to save the relationship.
