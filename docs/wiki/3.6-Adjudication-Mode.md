## 3.6	Adjudication Mode
1. Click the right-bottom to select Adjudication Mode to turn on the Adjudication Mode

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-1.PNG)

2. Then you will get a pop window like this below: Click Next

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-2.PNG)

3. Select Annotations by Annotator and then click Next

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-3.PNG)

   Or Select Annotations by Class and then click Next

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-4.PNG)

4. Define what are matches or non-matches

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-5.PNG)

5. Click Next to complete the processing

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-6.PNG)

6. Done.

   ![](https://github.com/chrisleng/ehost/blob/master/res/3.6-7.PNG)
